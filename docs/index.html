Classify a region, then nudge

  • Cells. Training coordinates are clustered into 160 regions with KMeans. Each region's reference point is snapped to a real training photo, so it never sits in the sea.
  • Offsets in kilometres, not degrees. A degree of longitude is ~86 km in Turkey but ~47 km in Iceland, so offsets are projected to local east/north km first.
  • Budget. 4,915,230 of the allowed 5,000,000 parameters. Trained only on the 11,758 provided images, 30 epochs, AdamW at lr 5e-5.

Honest numbers. Everything on this site comes from 5-fold cross-validation: every photo was predicted by a model that never saw it during training. That gives a 118.7 km median error. A single lucky validation split gave 103.5 km.
